# Research

One skill, vendored from the Hermes library: `grounded-citations`.

It is here because Shrimpy's whole design rests on the difference between a fact
that can be checked and one that merely sounds right. `aqua` owns the numbers,
the wiki cites its sources, and this covers the case where neither has the
answer and the reply would otherwise be the model's own recollection.
