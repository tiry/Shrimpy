# Iron

The one metric on the contaminant list with a legitimate reason to be present — but not one
to be raised casually here. Current values are `aqua status`; product rules are
`references/products.md`.

## What it is

An essential **plant micronutrient**, required for chlorophyll synthesis. Planted-tank
fertilisers supply it deliberately, usually in **chelated** form: bound to an organic
molecule that keeps it soluble and available, because free iron in oxygenated water at
aquarium pH precipitates out as insoluble oxide fairly quickly.

That precipitation is why an iron test can read near zero shortly after dosing while the
plants have taken up very little — the iron did not vanish, it fell out of solution.

Its place on the contaminant list is about **what it travels with** rather than its own
toxicity. Iron at planted-tank concentrations is not a danger to shrimp or snails; the
fertiliser carrying it may be, because comprehensive micronutrient mixes commonly include
copper. See `assets/wiki/metric/copper.md`.

## How it gets in

- **Plant fertilisers**, deliberately — the main source.
- **Aquasoil and iron-rich substrates**, leaching slowly.
- **Tap water** in some supplies.
- **Corroding metal** in equipment or decor.

## How it comes down

- **Plant uptake**, the intended route.
- **Precipitation** as oxide, which is the usual fate of the surplus.
- **Water changes**, for the dissolved fraction.
- **Stopping the source**, which for a planted tank means reviewing the dosing rather than
  removing iron.

## What not to do

**Do not dose iron to fix a deficiency you have not confirmed.** Yellowing new growth has
several causes, and `references/products.md` requires iron be checked before a fertiliser is
used for that reason.

**Do not use a fertiliser to raise hardness.** Iron and potassium salts raise TDS and EC
without supplying usable calcium or magnesium, and `references/products.md` prohibits this
specifically. See `assets/wiki/metric/gh.md`.

**Do not read a low iron result as proof the dose was too small.** Precipitation and uptake
both remove it between dosing and testing.
